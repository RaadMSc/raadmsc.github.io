01 / About

Civil engineering
with a materials perspective.

I am a Civil Engineer with an MSc in Structural Materials, combining engineering practice with advanced materials research and construction technology.

My professional interests connect structural materials, concrete technology, sustainable construction, and practical building solutions. I have hands-on experience with construction chemicals, concrete admixtures, waterproofing, insulation, and related building systems.

My research focuses on geopolymer materials, fire resistance, waste-derived materials, and sustainable alternatives to conventional cement-based construction.

“Connecting materials science, construction practice, and sustainable engineering.”
